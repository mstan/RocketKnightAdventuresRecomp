# Rocket Knight Adventures Recomp v0.1.12

Windows x64 ZIP and Linux x86_64 AppImage. Bring your own **Rocket Knight
Adventures (USA)** ROM (CRC32 `A6EFEC47`); no ROM is included.

This pass addresses the seven saves in the latest stage 1–7 report. The fire
corridor now uses the original placement range: the expanded scan could wake
its linked heat controller before the native camera reached it, leaving a
stack of fire sprites in view. The lava boss room keeps its native platform
and cave backdrop through one-pixel camera shake, and extends the platform
across the left widescreen margin. The shooter transition no longer paints a
short-lived dot field over the correctly rendered right side.

The large boss returns to the simpler right margin selected for this pass;
its edge can clip as it did in v0.1.9. In the later machine room, the fixed
wall is continued only while it is at the native viewport border. Once the
camera passes it, the earlier machinery fills the left margin. The flying
boss keeps its natural edge entry, and the Earth transition is unchanged.

All seven supplied saves were replayed in native and 16:9 modes, including
the transient shooter scene. The full renderer regression passed. Rewind,
the visual nine-slot save-state browser, adaptive widescreen, expanded enemy
activation and optional playtest cheats remain available.

Extract the Windows ZIP and run `RKARecomp.exe`, or run the Linux AppImage in
a writable folder. Choose **Mods > Widescreen > Adaptive (fit window)** and
**Expanded Spawn Range** for 16:9 playtesting.

Known limitations: the supplied fire-corridor save already contains objects
created by the early trigger, so the corrected activation timing needs a
fresh-run check. The machine-room margin still repeats a few background
details. A one-pixel propeller slice can appear as the flying boss enters at
the extreme edge, and the Earth transition retains its mirrored edge. Those
three cosmetic edges follow the playtester's choices for this release.
