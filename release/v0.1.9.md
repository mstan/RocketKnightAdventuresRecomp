# Rocket Knight Adventures Recomp v0.1.9

Windows x64 ZIP and Linux x86_64 AppImage. Bring your own **Rocket Knight
Adventures (USA)** ROM (CRC32 `A6EFEC47`); no ROM is included.

Widescreen fixes from the fourth playtest round:

- Plane-drawn bosses and the space-boss laser no longer show their far side
  in the opposite margin (stage 3 and stage 6 bosses, the airship).
- Locked boss rooms and the stage-1 bridge cover both sides with their own
  wall; actors just outside a locked room no longer draw over it.
- Vehicle and shooter enemies entering from the left are visible from the
  wide edge instead of appearing late (and firing while invisible).
- Stage-1 fire corridor embers start at the wide right edge.
- Crystal-cave lava and the chain room's swaying chains continue correctly
  across the margins while scrolling.
- The princess room continues its walls; the ending's floor and castle no
  longer stop at the 4:3 edges.

Runtime evidence files (`dispatch_misses.toml`, `floor_coverage.toml` and
friends) now accumulate across launches of the same build instead of being
reset at every start, and record sessions and frames covered. Please include
them with feedback.

Extract the Windows ZIP and run `RKARecomp.exe`, or run the Linux AppImage in a
writable folder. Use the launcher's ROM picker or pass the USA ROM path on the
command line. Enable **Mods > Widescreen**, select **Adaptive (fit window)**
or a fixed aspect ratio, and select **Expanded Spawn Range** under enemy
activation.
