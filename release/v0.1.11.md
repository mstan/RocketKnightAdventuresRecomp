# Rocket Knight Adventures Recomp v0.1.11

Windows x64 ZIP and Linux x86_64 AppImage. Bring your own **Rocket Knight
Adventures (USA)** ROM (CRC32 `A6EFEC47`); no ROM is included.

This release includes the widescreen fixes from the latest Part 1 and Part 2
save-state feedback. The stage-2 tank bridge uses its tile layout at the final
arena, avoiding repeated bridge edges. Several cutscene and lava-room margins
return to their simpler v0.1.9 presentation, so they no longer mirror Rocket
or other moving characters. The stage-1 boss room has solid walls on both
sides. In the later machine room, the left margin samples fixed wall art rather
than the live viewport edge; Rocket no longer repeats into that wall. The
large boss at the right edge uses one finite reflection instead of repeated
columns of machinery.

In the stage-3 fire corridor, the heat effect no longer wakes from the new
left margin, and scripted launcher pieces retain their native timing. Other
rightward placement scanning stays expanded. Replays of all supplied Part 1
and Part 2 saves passed in native and 16:9 modes, including moving left at the
machine-room wall. The full renderer regression passed. Rewind and the visual
nine-slot save-state browser from v0.1.10 remain available.

Extract the Windows ZIP and run `RKARecomp.exe`, or run the Linux AppImage in
a writable folder. Choose **Mods > Widescreen > Adaptive (fit window)** and
**Expanded Spawn Range** for 16:9 playtesting.

Known limitations: Part 1 save 8 briefly shows bright lines in the far-right
margin during the shooter transition. The fire corridor was validated by a
short save-state replay; its full fight still needs hands-on playtesting. The
large boss's continued right edge is an approximation of the original art.
